PILOT’S QUICK GUIDE

Get on their tail.

Think a move ahead. You have all the time you need.

  1. Choose a maneuver.

    Pick a card. Its arrow shows how you will move. The selected card turns green.

  2. Press the gold Fly button.

    Both planes move together. Your rival has already chosen in secret.

  3. Get a shot.

    Your guns fire automatically after moving. The target must be:

    Ahead of your noseWithin 280 mAt your altitude
The trick: aim for where the rival will finish, not where it is now. The map previews your move only.

Range & damage

Inside 110 m: 3 damage.
110–189 m: 2 damage.
190–280 m: 1 damage.

Altitude

Low, mid, or high. Climb and dive change one level. You must match the target’s level to fire.

Energy

Cruise, brake, and dive restore energy. Hard turns and climbing spend it. At 1 energy, recover before another hard move.

Reversals

Show reversals adds tight 150° turns. They cost 2 energy. A diving reversal turns 180° and drops one level.

Which difficulty should I choose?

Easy: the rookie expects straight flight and makes less consistent choices. Good for learning.
Normal: the rival weighs several possible moves.
Hard: the rival also watches your recent patterns and considers a second-turn response.
All levels use the same flying and firing rules. Select a level, then Start flight to begin a new sortie.

How does a flight end?

Dogfight: reduce the rival’s 10 hull points to zero.
First blood: be first to deal 3 total damage. Reaching it together is a draw.
Break contact: survive 8 turns with the rival starting on your tail. Bringing it down also wins.
Both down together, 40 turns, or both out of ammunition means a draw.

What do the stars mean?

Earn one star for a win, another for finishing with at least 7 hull, and another for winning within 8 turns. Practice never changes your record.

Three useful habits
Bank toward the rival before chasing it.
Use a reversal after passing each other.
Watch the rival’s firing line as well as your own.

Saving & replay

The status line tells you whether this browser saved the flight. Reopen the same file in the same browser to continue. Replay spends no ammunition and awards no extra points. A hosted version is needed for convenient iPhone Safari access.
